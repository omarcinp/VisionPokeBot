# Flash run: `pokebot goal "item ITEM_HM05 1"` on the emulator

First long autonomous goal on the emulator. Checkpoint at start:
`saves/state.json` + `saves/progress.json`, Route 4 (11,19), IVYSAUR Lv 18 +
PIDGEY, CATERPIE, RATTATA, Boulder Badge.

## Pre-flight: SIGTERM during planning

`timeout 120 pokebot goal … --dry-run` had to be SIGKILLed on an older
binary. The build on disk (22:14) predated commit 6f4e8d7 (22:20), which
added a 50 ms mirror thread copying the Ctrl-C flag into
`PlanOptions.stop`. The rebuilt binary honours SIGTERM (`ctrlc` with the
`termination` feature) and SIGINT: a `timeout -s TERM 4` dry run exits at
4.0 s with 124, the full dry run prints 36 steps and exits in 12.8 s.
Simplified in ef92941: `goal::run` takes the process's `Arc<AtomicBool>`
and hands it to the planner directly; the mirror thread is gone.

## Attempt 1: /tmp/flash-1

Plan 1 (36 steps, 67 min planned): Probe(trainer card) → Pewter Mart, 13
balls → catch SPEAROW, NIDORAN_M, MANKEY (Route 3), PARAS (Mt. Moon B1F),
GEODUDE (B2F), ZUBAT (1F), EKANS (Route 4) → Beat Miguel → Dome Fossil →
Nugget Bridge → Bill → grunt 5 → Probe(TM case) → S.S. Anne captain (HM01)
→ Train IVYSAUR to 22 → Misty → Teach Cut → Route 2 gatehouse aide (YES).

What happened: Probe(trainer card) read badges [1], 5 caught (first time
through the goal loop); Go(PewterCity_Mart) and Buy(13 POKE_BALL, ¥2600)
worked; Go(Route3) ended on the map's first tile (0,11) and the Catch hunt
walked to the grass at (15,9) itself.

Failure 1 (frame 12076, ~3.5 min in): a Route 3 trainer the story walk had
not fought spotted the player ("Excuse me! You looked at me, didn't you?").
The hunt declares `Expects::BATTLE`, which also covers dialogue, so the
interrupt wrapper never ran Unstick and `HuntStep` returned "letting the
scene settle" for ~1400 frames until the executor's nudge pressed B (frame
13492). The battle then started as a wild one: a ball was thrown at the
trainer's PIDGEY Lv9 (blocked), then it fought and won. Fix ed92089: the
hunt expects dialogue only while it plays a battle; `interrupt()` returns
early for a battle the step expects (its text and menus are the step's).
Fixture `captures/fixtures/emu-trainer-spotted-route3.png` (frame 12600),
test `a_trainers_challenge_during_a_catch_hunt_runs_unstick`.

The run continued on the old binary: wild PIDGEYs (caught flag known) are
fought off at ~25 s each while hunting SPEAROW.

Failure 2 (frame 31445): a SPEAROW Lv7 appeared and the catch policy
refused it ("the lead is at 39/60 HP, below 75 %"), while the hunt only
heals under 50 %: between the two bars every target encounter is wasted.
Fix c290875: a species hunt heals at `CATCH_MIN_HP` (75 %) and when the
lead has a status condition; training keeps 50 %.

Stopped at frame 39963 (13 battles, 0 caught; 11 min of play) to relaunch
with both fixes. Save point: PewterCity_Mart (4,3) after the purchase.
Recording deleted after analysis.

## Attempt 2: /tmp/flash-2

Relaunched from the Mart save with ed92089 + c290875.
Plan 1: 37 steps, 72 min (Route 3 SPEAROW, NIDORAN_M; Viridian Forest
WEEDLE; Diglett's Cave DIGLETT; Mt. Moon PARAS, GEODUDE…).

What happened: the trainer from attempt 1 was beaten, but a second Route 3
trainer challenged the player on the way to the grass (frame ~5100). The
new hunt `expects` worked: Unstick followed the intro, the battle ran as
the trainer's (Battle Auto, 95 s, ¥144 won). Then, the frame after
`BattleEnded`, Unstick failed "stuck waiting: conversation ending": the
drive loop keeps `waiting_since` across a handled interrupt, so the
conversation's wait counted the whole battle. The Catch failed once,
plan 2 (44 steps) started with a second Probe(trainer card), the hunt
resumed and caught SPEAROW at frame 26218 (4 balls; first autonomous catch
of the goal loop), saved at Route3 (34,12).
Fix ef1692c: a handled interrupt resets the step's wait. Test
`a_handled_interrupt_restarts_the_steps_wait` (drives a waiting step
through a battle frame with a 2-frame limit). Relaunched as flash-3 during
Go(MtMoon_B1F); recording deleted.

Noted, not yet fixed: every replan begins with Probe(trainer card) (8 s)
because the card's caught count is not stored (`pokedex_count_event`
needs both totals), so `PokedexCaught(≥10)` stays Unknown.
