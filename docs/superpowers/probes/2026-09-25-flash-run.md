# Flash run: `pokebot goal "item ITEM_HM05 1"` on the emulator

First long autonomous goal on the emulator. Checkpoint at start:
`saves/state.json` + `saves/progress.json`, Route 4 (11,19), IVYSAUR Lv 18 +
PIDGEY, CATERPIE, RATTATA, Boulder Badge.

## Pre-flight: SIGTERM during planning

`timeout 120 pokebot goal … --dry-run` had to be SIGKILLed on an older
binary. The build on disk (22:14) predated commit 6f4e8d7 (22:20), which
added a 50 ms mirror thread copying the Ctrl-C flag into
`PlanOptions.stop`. The rebuilt binary honours SIGTERM (`ctrlc` with the
`termination` feature) and SIGINT: a `timeout -s TERM 4` dry run exits at
4.0 s with 124, the full dry run prints 36 steps and exits in 12.8 s.
Simplified in ef92941: `goal::run` takes the process's `Arc<AtomicBool>`
and hands it to the planner directly; the mirror thread is gone.

## Attempt 6 analysis

The acts (events.jsonl, controller.jsonl) from (16, 17), the path's next
tile being (15, 17):

| frame | act | outcome | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17673 | Press Left | TimedOut after 71 frames | the player faced Down (he had just walked Down); a 160 ms press in a new direction only **turns** |
| 17745 | Press Left | Interrupted | Josh's "!" and walk; then the dialogue and the battle (17774–23564) |
| 23644 | Press Left | TimedOut after 71 frames | Josh stands on (15, 17); the second miss learnt the tile as blocked |
| 23716 | — | `Fail: looping` | the 4th act was never issued: `note_visit` fired before the replanned step around Josh |

Plan 2 (the same Go) started a **new** `GoStep`, so the learnt blocker was
gone (learned obstacles were per `Navigator`):

| frame | act | outcome | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25127 | Press Left | TimedOut | facing Left already: a real block (Josh) |
| 25199 | Press Left | TimedOut | second miss: (15, 17) learnt again |
| 25271 | Press Down | TimedOut | the detour's first step: facing Left, so this press only turned (contact sheet 25128–25311: the player faces Down at the end) |
| 25343 | — | `Fail: looping` | the 4th act (the real step Down) never issued |

So no NPC data or timing was wrong; four things were:

1. **A tap in a new direction only turns.** Every direction change in the
   whole run cost one 71-frame timeout plus a second tap (e.g. (17, 9) →
   (16, 9) at 17448 then Down at 17539, 91 frames apart). The walker's
   "first miss is probably a turn" rule handled it, but at the price of a
   full timeout — this is what looked like "inputs faster than the game
   accepts". `saves/timing.json` had `Turn` at 3 samples of ~15 ms: the
   `face` action confirms on `InputsDone`, so the model never measured a
   turn. Holds are fine (the game turns and walks within one hold).
2. **The pose-loop rule counted acts, not visits**, and on a map other
   than the destination's (`best` is only measured there) every act from
   the same tile counted: turn + blocked + turn = 3, and the detour's real
   step was the 4th, refused. Spec §8 says "visiting the same tile 4 times".
3. **Learnt blockers died with the leg.** The replanned `Go` began from
   nothing and repeated the same discovery.
4. **`IntentInfeasible` scoped to the whole `Go(MtMoon_B2F)`**: the second
   identical failure removed the only way to the fossil (and to every
   plan) for the session.

Josh's case generalises: a trainer who spots the player walks up and stays
adjacent for the rest of the visit to that map (the data position only
holds again after leaving and re-entering the map), so learnt blocks must
outlive the leg but be dropped on re-entering the map.

Fixes, commit 98d42df (regression tests: walker
`a_tap_in_a_new_direction_turns_first`,
`a_missed_tap_while_facing_the_way_learns_the_blocker`; nav
`a_blocked_npc_is_learnt_in_one_tap_after_a_turn_and_shared_with_the_next_leg`
(scripted from MtMoon_1F (16, 17) with the real world data); go
`four_arrivals_on_one_tile_without_progress_fail_the_leg`; goal
`a_go_failing_twice_stays_plannable`):

1. The walker asks for a **turn** (`Expectation::InputsDone`, `Turn`
   timing, 6-frame timeout) before a tap in a direction the player doesn't
   face; a tap he already faces along learns the blocker on its **first**
   miss. `facing` is forgotten on a map change (warps set it). The warp
   walk (`use_warp`'s last branch, which tapped every tile) now goes
   through the walker too: holds, turns first, the step onto the warp
   tile last.
2. The loop rule counts **arrivals** on a tile, not acts from it (the
   progress arrival counts as the first).
3. Blocked tiles live in a session store (`nav::BlockedTiles`, shared as
   `ToolContext::blocked`, seeded into every `Navigator` through
   `NavParts`), dropped when the map is re-entered (NPCs reset) and after
   15 min; logged as `TileBlocked` ("Tile MtMoon_1F (15, 17) blocked
   (learnt)").
4. `goal.rs`: a `Go` failing twice with the same reason is never marked
   infeasible; it logs the blocked tiles kept and replans.
